# Tag

Boxy (8px) uppercase label (`t-label` metrics) that names a section above its heading, or a category on a card.

- `ct-tag` — `surface-card` fill, `ink-muted` text, `primary` dot. Above left-aligned section headings.
- `ct-tag--plain` — no dot. Above centered headings.
- `ct-tag--on-photo` — translucent white with blur, `sky` dot. On photography and the hero.
- `ct-tag--dark` — on `midnight` cards and bands.

Keep it to 1–4 words. One tag per heading. It is a label, not a button: never make it clickable.

# StatItem

A figure and its caption, used four across in the `midnight` stats band (`ct-stats`).

- Numeral in `t-stat` (`on-dark`, tabular figures); a `+` or unit as `<sup>` in `sky`; caption in `t-caption`, `on-dark-muted`, max ~6 words.
- Items are divided by 1px white-14% rules; two per row below 900px.
- Numbers count up once (1.4s ease-out) when 60% visible; with reduced motion they show their final value.
- Only use figures the company can stand behind. Current figures come from the company profile: 0 fatal site injuries, 8 service lines, 16+ material brands, 25+ licensed activities — update them as the record grows.

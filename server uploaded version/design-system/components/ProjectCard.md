# ProjectCard

A whole-card link for one project: 4:3 photograph with a category tag, then the title, a one-line scope and a square arrow.

- Grid: `ct-projects` (3 columns, 20px gap). Lead with one `ct-project--lg` (spans 2 columns) beside one `ct-project--tall`, then rows of three. Tablet: 2 columns; mobile: 1 column, every image back to 4:3.
- Provide: a photograph, a category tag (`ct-tag--on-photo`, matching a service name), a title in `t-card-title`, a scope line under ~12 words, and the project link.
- Hover: image zooms to 1.04, the arrow box fills `midnight` and the arrow nudges up-right.
- The current titles are working titles written from the profile photos — replace them with real project names, clients and locations once they are approved.
